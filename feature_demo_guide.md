# SKEETER - Feature Demo Guide

If they ask to see the app in action, here's how to walk them through it and what to highlight.

---

## Pre-Demo Checklist

- [ ] Game is built and tested locally (runs without errors)
- [ ] You've played at least one full round (90 seconds)
- [ ] Audio is on (heartbeat is critical)
- [ ] Controller or keyboard is ready
- [ ] You know where the hotkeys are (H=briefing, F2=vertical capture, R=new round)

---

## The Demo Flow (10 minutes)

### 1. Launch & Show the Menu (1 minute)

**What you do:**
- Launch the game
- Show the menu with a bot match running behind it
- Click "Play"

**What you say:**
"Notice there's already a game running behind the menu - bots playing bots. This shows the game is always on, you just jump in. When you hit Play, you get assigned a random role."

**What they see:**
- A living room with three human bodies
- A mosquito flying around
- Blood visibly draining from humans
- The window glowing in the background

**Why it matters:**
- Instant visual hook: "I get what this game is in 3 seconds"
- Asymmetry is visible (three humans vs one mosquito)
- Tension is clear (mosquito has blood, humans try to swat)

---

### 2. Play One Full Round as Mosquito (3-4 minutes)

**How to play:**
- WASD to fly, Space/Ctrl for altitude
- Fly toward a human body
- Land on skin (automatic)
- Let it drink for a few seconds
- Notice your belly fills up (visual feedback)
- Notice your speed drops as belly fills
- Fly toward the window
- Try to reach it without getting swatted

**What you highlight:**

**First landing (15 seconds in):**
- "Notice my belly - it's starting to fill. The more I drink, the heavier I get."
- Let it fill to ~30%
- "At 30%, I'm still pretty fast. Let me keep drinking."

**Second bite (30 seconds in):**
- Bite another body part
- "The different body parts yield different amounts. An ear is much richer than a leg."
- Show the ear (drinks faster, more blood per second)

**Full belly (45 seconds in):**
- Get your belly to 70%
- "Watch my speed now. I'm moving way slower. The dash is locked out."
- Try to fly - visibly slower
- Try to hold Shift (dash doesn't activate)
- "This is the tension of the game: am I fast enough to escape, or do I need to bank soon?"

**Last Call (when you have 18 seconds left):**
- "This is the climax - everything I bank from here is worth 2x."
- Fly hard toward the window
- If you make it: "I reach the window, it flares, and I bank the blood. Points recorded."
- If you get swatted: "Death stops time and orbits the corpse. That's the failure state."

**Audio cues:**
- "Notice the heartbeat speeds up? That's your greed in audio. The fuller you are, the faster your heart beats."
- Point out when drinking sound changes (pitch varies by body part quality)
- Point out when bank chime plays (success state)

---

### 3. Play One Round as Host (3-4 minutes)

**How to play:**
- WASD to move, Mouse to look
- Click to swat at the mosquito
- Tab or 1/2/3 to jump between your three bodies
- The goal: stop the mosquito from reaching the window

**What you highlight:**

**First body jump (20 seconds in):**
- Switch from body 1 to body 2
- "See that momentary blindness? That's the cost of switching. You can't see while you jump, so you have to predict where the mosquito is."

**Strategic positioning (45 seconds in):**
- Position yourself between mosquito and window
- "I'm trying to trap it. Three bodies give me coverage - I can be in multiple places."

**Missed swat (if it happens):**
- "Whoosh - I missed! Notice the slow-motion and the camera punch? And it says 'MISSED BY 6 CM'."
- This is designed for clips - that moment is worth watching

**Successful swat (if you get one):**
- Click when mosquito is close
- "Got it! Death stops time and orbits the corpse."
- Show the ragdoll (legs separate, wings flutter)

**Talking (optional, if demo allows):**
- Hold V to talk
- "My voice comes out of my body in 3D space. If I talk near the mosquito, I'm giving away my position."
- Point out the proximity indicator

---

### 4. Show Accessibility Features (1 minute)

**Press Esc to open Settings:**
- Show "Reduce motion" toggle
  - "This disables slow-mo, camera punches, and freeze-frames. Useful for motion sickness."
- Show "Color blindness" mode
  - "The two most important colors (blood and window) are red and green. This mode moves them to cyan."
- Show "Interface scaling"
  - "Make HUD bigger or smaller without affecting gameplay."
- Show "Audio devices"
  - "You can pick which microphone to use, and there's a live input meter."

**What you say:**
"Accessibility wasn't bolted on - it's designed in from the start. These aren't 'handicap modes,' they're just options. Everyone should be able to play and enjoy this."

---

### 5. Show Vertical Capture (1 minute)

**While in a round, press F2:**
- Game reframes to 9:16 (portrait)
- HUD stays inside the crop
- "This is for content creators. Streamers can record in vertical format at record time, not crop afterwards. Perfect for TikTok / YouTube Shorts."

**Highlight:**
- "The game watches itself and helps creators. Vertical capture, highlight logging, recap cards - all tools for clip makers."

---

### 6. Show Personal Bests (end of round)

**After you die or bank:**
- Look at the recap card
- "Every stat you beat gets a 'NEW BEST' flag. This drives players to come back - there's always a number to beat."
- Show: highest score, closest call, bank streak, etc.

**What you say:**
"No progression system, no unlocks, no battle pass. Just you versus your own personal bests. It's simple, and it works."

---

## Talking Points During Demo

### "Why this is interesting" (what to emphasize):

**Core Loop:**
"The whole game is 90 seconds and the same question every round: do I take one more bite or run it home now? That tension is the entire game."

**Asymmetry:**
"Roles flip every round, so you never get bored. As a mosquito you're trying to escape. As a host you're trying to trap. Completely different games."

**Design for Content:**
"Notice how every dramatic moment is designed to be watchable. Near-miss slow-mo, freeze-frame on death, vertical capture for video. This game was designed with streamers in mind."

**Accessibility:**
"I didn't add accessibility as an afterthought. It's baked in. Different doesn't mean worse."

## Common Q&A During Demo

### Q: "Can I play this with friends?"

**Answer:**
"Yes. You can play 2-4 player online via LAN or with port forwarding for internet. Empty seats are filled by bots at whatever difficulty you set, so even 2-player games feel full."

**If they ask about latency:**
"For LAN it's instant. For internet it depends on ISP, but typical ping is 50-100ms, which is fine for a 90-second round. Voice chat is the same - there's always a little delay but it works."

---

### Q: "Is there progression or unlocks?"

**Answer:**
"Nope. No battle pass, no cosmetics, no leveling system. The progression is personal bests - beat your own score, find longer streaks. It's simple and keeps people coming back."

**If they ask about monetization:**
"v1.0 is $20 one-time purchase. No DLC, no cosmetics, no seasonal battle pass. The game is designed to be complete on day one."

---

### Q: "How long does a full session last?"

**Answer:**
"Rounds are 90 seconds. Best of 5 = 7-8 minutes. People usually play 2-3 matches (15-20 minutes) in a session. It's perfect for streaming, or just a quick break."

---

### Q: "What about bots - are they good?"

**Answer:**
"They're handicapped intentionally. Easy bots are slow to react and hesitate. Hard bots are aggressive but still follow the same rules (voice gives away position, wingbeat is audible). You can tune difficulty per match."

**Demo moment:** 
"Watch the hard bot - it positions itself between me and the window. It's not perfect AI (no pathfinding), but it makes good decisions."

---

## Demo Failure Modes (How to Recover)

### "Game crashes on launch"

- "Looks like there's a build issue. Let me rebuild it real quick." (5 minutes)
- Show the codebase while waiting: "Meanwhile, here's the architecture - 75 files, event-driven design..."
- Do a code walkthrough instead

### "I'm really bad at the controls (getting swatted immediately)"

- "Okay, let me switch to easy bots and show you a successful round where I actually make it to the window."
- Or: "Let me play as the host so you can see the other side."

### "Multiplayer isn't set up"

- "Multiplayer requires port forwarding, which we don't have in this environment. But I can show you the networking code - it's pretty clean."
- Show `NetSession.cs`, explain the intent abstraction

### "Audio isn't working"

- "The audio synthesis is running (you can see I'm drinking), but speakers might be muted. Let me show you the audio code instead."
- Point to `ProceduralAudio.cs`: "All the sounds - heartbeat, slap, bank chime - are synthesized at runtime from algorithms, no audio files."

## Ending the Demo (1 minute)

**Recap what they saw:**

"SKEETER is a 90-second asymmetric party game where the core tension - blood is heavy - drives every design decision. Multiplayer, voice chat, bot fill, visual feedback for content creators, accessibility from day one, and a simple 'beat your own best' progression system. 75 files, 13K lines of C#, peer-to-peer networking, and a design that's tight enough that every feature serves the core loop."

**Ask them:**

"Do you have any questions about the architecture, the multiplayer design, or how I approached the balance?"

---

## After the Demo

**What they might ask:**

**"Can you add X feature?"**
- "Sure. The event-driven architecture makes it easy. For example, if you wanted a screenshake indicator [new feature], I'd add a handler to GameEvents.OnScreenshake. One file, no touching gameplay code."

**"How would you optimize this for mobile?"**
- "The architecture is platform-agnostic. Unity supports iOS/Android. The main optimization would be touch controls (tap instead of WASD), and maybe reducing particle count. The core loop doesn't change."

**"What if you wanted ranked play?"**
- "You'd add a backend leaderboard (simple database + REST API) and switch from peer-to-peer to server-authoritative outcomes. That's documented in the Roadmap. The intent system makes that feasible."

---

## One Final Tip

If the demo goes well and they seem impressed, say:

"The best part of this project was how tight the core loop is. Everything - audio, particles, physics, AI - serves the tension. Nothing is wasted. That's what I'm going for in my next project too."

This shows you understand design discipline, not just code chops.

**Good luck with the demo.**
